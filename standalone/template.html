HireHubResume check

See where your resume fits, before you apply.

Upload your resume. A trained model predicts your best-fit roles, your skills are matched against 70 jobs, and you get a list of what to learn next.

Or paste the text of your resume

Your file is read inside this page. It is not uploaded or stored anywhere.

How this works. Roles come from a TF-IDF + logistic regression classifier. Job scores combine skill coverage (60%), text similarity (25%) and CGPA fit (15%), the same method as the full HireHub app.

Limits. The classifier was trained on synthetic resumes, so treat its roles as a guide. Job listings are sample data. The full app adds accounts, recruiter ranking, ChromaDB vector search and a chat assistant.
